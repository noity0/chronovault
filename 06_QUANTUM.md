## 1. REAL QUANTUM HARDWARE, KEYLESS — ANU QRNG
- Australian National University ka **Quantum Random Number Generator** public API:
  vacuum me photon fluctuations ko measure karke asli QUANTUM random bits deta hai.
- Use: **Multiverse branch seeding** (alternate timeline ka quantum seed), quantum
  dice, sampling. Label system: `QUANTUM (ANU)` → `ATMOSPHERIC (random.org)` →
  `CSPRNG (os.urandom)` — machine hamesha batayegi randomness kis tier se aayi.
- Ye sabse pehli baar hai jab kisi personal time machine me **asli quantum
  hardware** se timelines seed ho rahi hain.

## 2. RELATIVITY ENGINE (REAL physics, exact math)
- **Lorentz factor** `γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)` — proper time vs Earth time conversions.
- **GPS correction** (real numbers): velocity effect −7.2 μs/day, gravitational
  effect +45.9 μs/day → net **+38.6 μs/day**. Machine me yehi formula hai.
- **Twin paradox calculator**: speed/altitude dalo → "Earth pe X saal, tumpe Y".
- **Gravitational time dilation**: `dτ/dt ≈ 1 + gh/c²` (weak field) — altitude
  dalo, asli microseconds dikhenge.
- Honest scale: ye effects microscopic hain, par MATH 100% real hai — same
  equations jo GPS satellites roz use karte hain.

## 3. ARROW OF TIME — Shannon Entropy Engine

## 4. MINKOWSKI LIGHTCONES — Causal Horizon Analysis (futuristic + real)
